1. Why Single Agents Fail at Biology Reproduction, and How POPPER Fixes Them

One formatting or sign error zeroes out an entire table. A separate statistical Critic catches and repairs these bugs before grading.

Why single agents fail: Reproducing a biology study from raw DNA/RNA sequencing files takes 30+ steps. BixBench3 grades each output table by multiplying Row Match, Column Match, and Value Correlation together. When a single agent gets the gene values 99.9% right but leaves a .15 version tag on gene IDs or flips the control group, one term hits 0.00 and the entire table scores zero.

Separating builder from critic: Instead of letting one agent grade its own code, we added an independent Critic based on POPPER sequential e-value testing (Huang et al., ICML 2025). The Critic tests every draft table for formatting, p-value, and biological sign errors while keeping false alarms below 5%.

The result on raw data: Across 19 tables rebuilt from 113 raw FASTQ files (27.3 GB), our Critic caught all 13 broken Round-1 tables and sent exact repair instructions to the Experimenter, lifting our overall score from 0.1615 to 0.7413.

2. Why Our Critic Needed Exa Search and past.dev Shared Memory

A Critic cannot verify biology without external literature facts (Exa) or remember dataset rules across 40 minutes of compute (past.dev).

Why Exa Search was essential (+57.0 pts overall): First, Task 9 used a specialized 3' Tag-Seq protocol absent from LLM training weights. Without Exa, our swarm guessed standard RNA settings and lost 85% of sequencing reads (scoring 0.0254). Exa found the exact GitHub repository (z0on/tag-based_RNAseq) showing we needed forward-stranded counting (-s 1) and a 5'-barcode clipper. Second, the Critic used Exa (blocking the target paper's DOI) to look up textbook marker genes (SNAP25, SYP) and prove that Round 1's control group was backwards.

Why past.dev Memory was essential (+26.3 pts overall): Processing 113 raw FASTQ files takes over 40 minutes, causing agents to forget early dataset rules. Our Inspector stored exact sample rules in past.dev at minute 1 ("merge 28 sequencing runs into 16 biological samples" and "drop the 89th summary row"), and our Auditor wrote rejected bugs into permanent global_learnings so later runs avoided Round-1 mistakes.

3. Controlled 5-Way Ablation Across 19 Rebuilt Tables

Evaluated with the official bixbench3 grade CLI and the GPT-5.5 PhDval-1 process judge.

4.6× over single-agent baseline: Under identical compute, a single agent scores 0.1615. Adding our 5-agent swarm with Exa search, past.dev shared memory, and POPPER falsification reaches 0.7413 across 19 artifacts (0.7302 task-mean) and 0.8450 on the official GPT-5.5 PhDval-1 judge.

Transparent scope note: The 0.5230 Edison Scientific leaderboard record is averaged across all 20 studies (138 tables, ~150+ GB of raw sequencing data). Within hackathon compute, we ran full raw-FASTQ reproduction and 5-way ablations on a 2-study, 19-table slice (27.3 GB raw FASTQ: Task 9 at 0.7536 and Task 11 at 0.7068).

Why 4 pathway tables scored 0.20–0.47: Our count matrices hit 0.99+ correlation and DESeq2 tables hit 0.86–0.97. Our only lower scores come from 2026 vs 2021 Bioconductor Gene Ontology database drift. All logs and rejections are live at http://127.0.0.1:8765.

Appendix A2: POPPER 6-Stage Sequential E-Value Falsification Checks

Vovk-Wang calibration (e_k = 0.5 * p_k^-0.5) and Ville's inequality (E_t = ∏ e_k ≥ 20.0) bound false rejections below 5%.
POPPER Check Question Tested by Critic E-Value on Fail Repair Instruction Sent to Experimenter
F1: File & Size Does the file exist, parse cleanly, and have a realistic row count? × 500.0 Re-run alignment/counting script and verify output path.
F2: Column Names Do column headers match exact sample names and required order? × 500.0 Drop tool metadata columns and map sample sheet names.
F3: Gene ID Format Do row IDs match target format (clean ENSG vs Probe ID)? × 500.0 Strip .version decimals on DEG tables; keep on raw counts.
F4: Math Sanity Are there NaN cells or rows where +log2FC has a −stat? × 250.0 Drop incomplete rows and recompute from unified model.
F5: P-Value Filter Are all adjusted p-values < 0.05 and fold-changes above cutoff? × 250.0 Apply Benjamini-Hochberg padj < 0.05 and |log2FC| filter.
F6: Real Biology Do textbook positive-control genes move in the right direction? × 500.0 Relevel DESeq2 control group so fold-change signs flip.
