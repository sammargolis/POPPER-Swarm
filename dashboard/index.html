POPPER Sequential Falsification Ledger ("What the Swarm Rejected")

Extending POPPER (Huang et al., ICML 2025) to executable bioinformatics contracts: every candidate artifact is subjected to 6 sequential refutation experiments (F1_EXISTENCE – F6_BIO_PRIOR_ALIGNMENT).

Anytime-Valid Sequential E-Process & Ville's Inequality

For each candidate artifact, the POPPER Critic tests the null hypothesis H0: "Artifact satisfies all structural, statistical, and biological invariants". Each falsification test k ∈ {1..6} yields a p-value pk against validity, converted via the Vovk-Wang (2021) calibrator into an e-value:

1. Vovk-Wang Calibrator
ek = ½ · pk−1/2
ek ≤ 1 when pk ≥ 0.25
2. E-Process Martingale
Et = ∏k=1..t ek
Accumulates across checks
3. Ville's Stopping Rule
Et ≥ 1/α = 20.0
Type-I error ≤ α = 0.05
